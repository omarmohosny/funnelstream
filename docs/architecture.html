FunnelStream, from a click to a decision

A quick-commerce style event pipeline. Customer events and orders flow through Kafka and a Bronze and Silver lake. One lane feeds live Grafana dashboards within a minute. The other builds trusted daily tables in Databricks for Power BI. The new inventory work adds stock, express delivery and campaign insight on top of the same pipeline. All inventory data is simulated.

What each user gets

Operations

Grafana, live

  • 1Stock now, per store and product. See which of the 8 dark stores has run out of which product, and how many lines are low.
  • 2Demand that met an empty shelf. Views and add-to-carts on products with zero stock, ranked, with the product name.
  • 3Express promise watch. Every express order carries its own 20-minute promise. Orders at risk, breaches in the last hour, on-time rate and the slowest orders are on one panel.
  • 4Alerts that point to an action. Demand on an out-of-stock product warns. Any single breach of the promise is critical, with a runbook.
Value: restock or reroute before customers give up, and catch a late delivery while it can still be saved.

Marketing

Power BI and Grafana, daily

  • 1ROAS delivered, not only placed. Revenue from cancelled, returned and failed cash-on-delivery orders is removed, so a campaign that looks strong on placed revenue is judged on cash.
  • 2Platform conversions against attributed orders. Shows how much the ad platform claims compared with what the order data supports.
  • 3Estimated spend exposed to out-of-stock. Spend times the share of campaign clicks that landed on an empty shelf. A planning estimate, never called wasted.
  • 4Funnel drop-off. Where sessions are lost between view, cart, checkout and order, by channel and platform.
Value: shift budget away from campaigns pointing at products that cannot be served, and stop paying for traffic that cannot convert.

The data team

Confidence, repeatable

  • 1Replay safety. Silver MERGEs by key, so restarting or replaying Kafka does not create duplicates. A duplicate check covers every key table.
  • 2Contracts at the door. Bad events go to quarantine with a reason, not into the numbers.
  • 3One command to assess everything. The smoke test walks 15 stages from the simulator to Power BI sources in an isolated lake and database, and writes a report.
  • 4Tested. 998 host tests pass, and the new Spark tests ran on a real engine on 11 October.
Value: a defense answer to "how do you know it is right?" that points at checks, not trust.

Read the numbers with these limits

Inventory is simulated.Reservations are out of scope. Stock is taken when an order is placed and is not returned if the order is cancelled.
The out-of-stock flag is the stock when the minute was written.A replay can flag a minute differently. Dashboards say so.
Two day definitions.Ad spend, orders and demand all follow the Cairo day.
Newest days are provisional.Orders take 2 to 4 days to settle, so delivered revenue for recent days is incomplete.

Why the design holds up

Idempotent by designKeys and MERGE on every Silver and Gold table. Aggregates are recomputed, never incremented.
Privacy lineOnly allow-listed columns reach Databricks. Grafana uses a read-only role limited to named tables.
Honest ratiosEvery Power BI rate is a ratio of sums. Spend-day logic stops ROAS dividing all revenue by two days of spend.
IsolationThe smoke test and benchmark refuse to touch the demo lake, the real catalog or the demo database.
